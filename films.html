the slate

Three lanes, one roof.

Every Haniko project lives in one of three lanes, each rooted in the same instinct for precision and reach.

Kurdish Ground

Documentary & narrative, rooted at home

Films centered on Kurdish communities, history, and contemporary life, across Kurdistan and the diaspora. This is the foundation of the studio: stories that don't need to be translated to matter, only to be told well.

Bridge Works

Co-productions across cultures

Dual-culture stories pairing Kurdish narratives with other regions, communities, and artistic traditions, built through active co-production and collaboration, not a single studio working alone.

Verse to Screen

Poetry, translated into image

Short-form work that carries literary voices, including Kochar's own, onto the screen. A space for formal risk, restraint, and the kind of image that does what explanation can't.
