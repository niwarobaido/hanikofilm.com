What we make

Haniko's slate sits across three lanes, all rooted in the same instinct for precision and reach.

Kurdish Ground

Documentaries and narrative films rooted in Kurdish communities, history, and contemporary life, across Kurdistan and the diaspora.

Bridge Works

Co-productions and dual-culture stories pairing Kurdish narratives with other regions, cultures, and artists.

Verse to Screen

Short-form, poetic work translating literary voices, including Kochar's own, into visual storytelling.
